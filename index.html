EcoShield live demo

AI talks, code decides, a human approves.

What runs here: EcoShield's real core.js (the same file our tests run) in your browser, on synthetic data: leak detector, evidence strength score (0 to 100) that sets how much the agent may do alone, bill forecaster on DEWA's published tariff (simplified), heat-safety guard, offline-room-sensor handling, privacy filter, input checks and the single write gateway. What does not: the AI planner and the AI-written explanation, which run in our local n8n workflow (screenshots are in the presentation). Nothing on this page calls an AI or sends data anywhere.
